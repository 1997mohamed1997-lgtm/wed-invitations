Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

Wed Invitations provides digital wedding invitations and personalized guest communication. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be used when invitations are prepared and sent to guests.

Information We Use

Depending on the invitation service, we may process:

  • Guest name
  • WhatsApp/mobile phone number
  • Personalized invitation image or invitation details
  • Guest response to the invitation, such as confirmation or apology

How We Use Information

Information is used only for purposes related to the invitation service, including preparing personalized invitations, sending invitations through WhatsApp, and recording guest responses such as attendance confirmation or apology.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the invitation service, such as hosting, messaging, and technical infrastructure providers.

Data Retention and Deletion

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide and manage the invitation service. A guest or the person who provided the information may request deletion of information associated with an invitation.

To request deletion, please contact the person or organization that arranged the invitation and ask them to have the associated guest information removed.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used for the invitation service. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or a request concerning personal information, please contact the organizer or service provider that sent your invitation.
